## Reflection on AI Usage

AI was highly effective for structuring the project and accelerating the drafting process, but it required active oversight. Initial outputs often lacked specificity and included weak or generic sources, which were not suitable for academic work.

The most important part of the process was verification and refinement. By iterating on drafts, replacing sources, and validating claims, I ensured the final deliverable was accurate and credible.

This project demonstrated that AI is a valuable tool for productivity, but it must be used critically. Strong results depend on human judgment, validation, and iterative improvement rather than relying on AI outputs alone.
